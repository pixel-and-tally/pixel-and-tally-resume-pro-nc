---
title: "\"Thank You\" for the Interview"
date: 2017-03-14
categories: ["Interview Tips"]
---

Sending a thank-you note is a great way to stand out after an interview, and it can position you as the ideal candidate long after you have left the room. I highly suggest doing this after every interview (unless you are absolutely not interested in the position).

**Use email.** Snail mail represents the ultimate formality and personal touch for important matters, but with today's faster interview and hiring process, email has become the accepted standard.

**Be timely.** Timing is everything in the interview game. There's no better way to show your appreciation for the interview and your interest in the position than a thoughtful follow-up. Send your thank-you within 24 hours of the interview.

**Keep it short and sweet.** In a world where a resume gets only 6 seconds of a hiring manager's time, you can be certain your thank-you note will get a similar scan. Don't be verbose and risk turning off the hiring manager with what will be your final impression before the job offer stage.

**Thank everyone.** Time is valuable for every interviewer who meets with you. Assume all of them will weigh in on whether to hire you. Whatever you do, do not copy and paste the same note to each person. Colleagues talk, and there's nothing worse than being the topic of the copy-and-paste thank-you conversation.

If you follow these simple steps, you'll know you have done everything possible to make the most professional impression and be remembered. Good luck!
