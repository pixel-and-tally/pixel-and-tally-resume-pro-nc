---
title: "7 Interview Tips That Will Help You Get the Job"
date: 2017-09-10
categories: ["Interview Tips", "Job Search"]
---

These job interview tips will help you prepare for successful interviews. Proper preparation will put you at ease and relieve some of the stress. The more you prepare, the more comfortable and successful you will be.

**1. Practice and prepare.** Practice your responses to the typical interview questions most employers ask. Think of concrete examples you can use to highlight your skills. The easiest way is to list the job requirements and match them to your experience. Providing evidence of your success and experience is a great way to promote yourself. Also have a list of your own questions ready to ask the employer.

**2. Research the company.** Do your homework on the employer and industry so you are ready for the question "What do you know about this company?" Try to relate what you've learned about the company when answering questions. Know the interviewer's name and use it during the interview. If you're not sure of the name, call and ask before your interview. Building rapport and making a personal connection with your interviewer can improve your chances of getting hired. People tend to hire candidates they like, and who seem to fit the company culture.

**3. Get ready ahead of time.** Don't wait until the last minute to prepare. Have one good interview outfit ready so you can interview on short notice without worrying about what to wear. When you have an interview lined up, get everything ready the night before. Make sure your interview attire is neat, tidy, and appropriate for the type of firm you are interviewing with. Bring a nice portfolio with extra copies of your resume, plus a pen and paper for notes.

**4. Be on time (that means early).** On time means five to ten minutes early. If need be, drive to the interview location ahead of time so you know exactly where you are going and how long it takes to get there. Give yourself a few extra minutes to visit the restroom, check your outfit, and calm your nerves.

**5. Try to stay calm.** During the interview, try to relax and stay as calm as possible. Remember that your body language says as much about you as your answers. Proper preparation will let you exude confidence. Take a moment to regroup if you need it. Maintain eye contact with the interviewer. Listen to the entire question before you answer, and pay attention. You will be embarrassed if you forget the question.

**6. Show what you know.** Relate what you know about the company when answering questions. When discussing your career accomplishments, match them to what the company is looking for. Use examples from your research, for instance: "I noticed that when you implemented a new software system last year, your customer satisfaction ratings improved dramatically. I am well versed in the latest technologies from my experience developing software at ABC, and I appreciate a company that strives to lead its industry."

**7. Follow up.** Always follow up with a thank-you note or email restating your interest in the position. You can also include any details you forgot to mention during the interview. If you interview with multiple people, send each one a personal note. Send your thank-you within 24 hours of your interview.
