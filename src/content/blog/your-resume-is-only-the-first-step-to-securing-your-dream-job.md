---
title: "Your Resume Is Only the First Step to Securing Your Dream Job"
date: 2017-02-24
categories: ["Job Search"]
---

A lot of people believe that once they submit a professional resume, all they need to do is wait for the phone to ring or for an email inviting them to interview. Unfortunately, it doesn't always happen that way. Don't get discouraged. Stay active in your job search rather than becoming passive.

**Don't give up, follow up.** Keep a spreadsheet of when you sent your resume out. If you have not heard anything in two weeks, send a follow-up letter stating your continued interest in the company and position. Attach your resume again. Show the hiring manager how your skills, knowledge, and abilities align with the company's mission. Educate yourself about their products and services so you can mention them specifically in your letter. Give it another two weeks, and if you haven't heard back by then, it's probably time to move on.

**Show interest, not aggression.** No hiring manager wants to be bombarded with emails or phone calls from a desperate applicant. Temper your enthusiasm with respect for the hiring manager. Not everyone can be hired, nor can the hiring manager respond to everyone who was not chosen. Again, move on.

**Never stay still.** Never put all your eggs in one basket. Even if you feel confident that you are going to be hired at a particular company, keep looking, submitting your resume, networking, updating your LinkedIn profile, and attending job fairs. Keep your skills up to date and continue to acquire new ones that will help you land a better position.

Do not stop looking until you have that job offer in hand!
