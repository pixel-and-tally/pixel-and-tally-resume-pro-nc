Research on salary negotiation has shown that as many as 4 out of 5 companies are willing to negotiate compensation. However, only a small percentage of job candidates actually negotiate with a prospective employer.

Job offer negotiations can feel intimidating, but you don't have to be an expert negotiator to negotiate a job offer successfully. Simply knowing the basic rules and strategies means you can prepare, plan your negotiation properly, and feel comfortable with the process.

Negotiating your salary has two major stages. The first is preparation and planning. The second is the actual negotiation, where communication and information sharing take place.

The preparation and planning stage involves researching, gathering, and analyzing information and planning your negotiation strategy. Take these 5 easy steps to a successful negotiation.

**1. Determine your competitive market value.** Knowing what you are worth in the marketplace is essential to effective job offer negotiation. It gives you a benchmark for any offer you receive.

**2. Determine your value to this company.** Understand what you are worth to this specific company and how much bargaining power you really have. How much does this company need your skills right now?

**3. Calculate your acceptable salary range.** You want to earn enough to support your lifestyle. Use an expenses calculator to find out how much money you need to live on and how much you would like to live on. Those two amounts give you your acceptable salary range.

**4. Evaluate the entire compensation package, including benefits.** Look at both direct and indirect benefits. Work out the dollar value of these benefits and add it to the base salary for a more accurate picture of the total compensation.

**5. Plan your actual negotiation** using all the information you have gathered.

Following each step diligently, with a confident attitude, will increase your chances of a successful outcome. Good luck!
