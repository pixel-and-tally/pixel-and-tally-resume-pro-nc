---
title: "Tips for Working With a Recruiter"
date: 2017-05-24
categories: ["Job Search", "Career Planning"]
---

If you are looking for a new job or contract opportunity and think a recruiter may be able to help, here are three basic tips to get you started.

**Get your foot in the door.** The best way to get noticed by a recruiter is through a referral from a networking contact or acquaintance in your industry. Cold calling an agency may not give you the results you want. Have your referral call or email the recruiter, or send the email yourself and mention who referred you. If you're new to the area and don't know anyone, join an organization in your industry to meet people. Most savvy recruiters pay particular attention to newcomers. They want the chance to represent new talent before their competitors do.

**Answer your phone.** What's the best way to get on your recruiting agency's short list? Aside from having a killer resume, how you handle the call from your recruiter is of utmost importance. Answer the call, or return it as soon as possible. Recruiters are busy and want to present a great candidate to a client right away.

When your recruiter tells you about a position, listen carefully and take notes. If the position isn't a fit for your skills, share names from your network to help them find the right candidate. Many agencies will pay you for successful referrals. In return, your recruiter will keep looking for the right client for your skills and experience. Working with a recruiter is a two-way street, and building rapport will pay off in the long haul.

**Follow up.** Staying top of mind ensures you are one of the first people your recruiter thinks of when a job or project request comes in. Call or email to check in at regular intervals, every couple of weeks or at least once a month. Many candidates wonder why they interviewed at an agency and never heard back. The more engaged you are with your recruiter, the better your chance of getting called for work. Keep your resume updated and let your recruiter know when it changes. Your new experience or software knowledge may be exactly what the next client is looking for.

Don't give up, and stay in touch with your recruiter!
