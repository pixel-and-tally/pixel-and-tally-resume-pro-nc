---
title: "What Is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?"
date: 2018-09-03
categories: ["Resume Tips"]
---

An applicant tracking system (ATS) is a software application that handles recruitment electronically. These systems are often called "robots" or "robot scanners."

Applicant tracking systems ease the workload of recruiters and hiring managers. The internet makes it fast and easy for job seekers to apply for jobs. In fact, job boards like Monster, Indeed, and other online job portals let job seekers apply for a job using a saved resume with only one click.

Research on these sites shows that for every corporate job posting there are up to 250 applications. Of these candidates, only 4 to 6 will be contacted for an interview. Imagine the cost and workload of screening these applications manually!

An ATS is usually the first part of the screening process, since it lets the recruiter or employer process large numbers of applications efficiently and rank how well each applicant matches the requirements of the job.

Recruiters decide on the keywords for each position and program the ATS to look for them. Applications that do not have the keywords will not move on to the next stage of the recruitment process.

The trick for candidates is identifying the keywords and phrases the employer requires. One of the easiest ways to do this is to work the keywords used in the job posting or position description into your resume.

Times have changed. Not only do you need the desired skills, experience, and qualifications, you also need to format and write your resume to get past the robot scanners searching for keywords in your application.

The bottom line: no keywords, no interview.
