Finding a job can be a difficult task, and the way to do it has changed significantly over the years. Many of us are used to searching for a position in the newspaper, online, or through employment agencies. Those are ways of the past! The best way to find out about job openings is through networking and word of mouth. The following are top tips to help you network your way to employment.

**Why network?** Most jobs are never posted, but are instead filled by referral. To make sure you're contacted by the hiring manager, you must make a connection. If you don't, you won't come to mind when a position opens up.

**What to do.** Make a list of people (your network). The list includes anyone you know: family, friends, co-workers, teachers, classmates, neighbors, your kid's friends' parents, and so on. If you really think about it, we are networking almost every day. When you're waiting for your oil change and you strike up a conversation with the person next to you, that's networking!

**Tap into the network.** Go through your list and note anyone who might be able to help in your search. Reach out and let them know you're looking for a new position or would like to make connections. This will definitely help grow your network.

**Attend conferences.** Find industry-specific conferences, support groups, church events, or any social function. Prepare in advance and know who will be attending and who you might want to connect with. Come prepared with your resume polished and ready to go. I do not suggest handing it out at every function unless it's a career fair or you're specifically asked for it.

You should also do some digging on LinkedIn and make valuable connections there. Don't go hog wild and start reaching out to everyone, but make sure the connections mean something and will add true value to your job search. You may need one of your connections to make a warm introduction on LinkedIn to help guide that connection.

Good luck in your networking, and keep in mind that the job may not come overnight, but the effort is well worth it!
