---
title: "\"Smart\" Questions to Ask During Your Interview"
date: 2017-10-21
categories: ["Interview Tips", "Job Search"]
---

"So, do you have any questions for me?" Just about every interview ends with this question, and your answer should always be an emphatic "Yes, I'm glad you asked."

By the time you've reached the interview, you've shown that your strengths line up with the company's needs. If you don't have smart questions prepared, you risk the interviewer assuming you're not interested or didn't bother to prepare.

Your chance to ask questions usually comes at the end of the interview. Prepare at least two questions that show your interest in the position, your drive to excel in the role, and that you've done your homework on the company, industry, and interviewer.

The best questions are focused and open-ended. Avoid yes-or-no questions, as well as questions so broad they are difficult to answer. You don't want to stump the interviewer when you're trying to make a good impression and build rapport.

Still not sure what to ask? Here are some good examples:

1. Can you tell me more about the day-to-day responsibilities of this job?
2. What do you think are the most important qualities for someone to excel in this role?
3. What are your expectations for this role in the first 30 days, 60 days, and year?
4. How would you describe the culture of the company?
5. Where do you think the company is headed in the next 5 years?
6. Who do you consider your top competitor, and why?
7. What do you like best about working for this company?
8. What is the typical career path for someone in this role?
9. What are the next steps in the interview process?

Whatever you're curious about, take full advantage of this part of the interview and shine!
